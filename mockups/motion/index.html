Five ways to move

Each one keeps the sky, the gold and your words, and adds one idea. The first three build on the site you have; the last two are bigger redos. Use the bar at the top right to jump between them.

1Builds on now

Day

The scroll is one day. A single sun keeps you company from the sunrise at the top to the sunset at the end, and the sky changes colour with it.

Try: scroll slowly to the bottom.

Open Day 2Builds on now

Sun

One thing to hold. Drag the sun and the whole page changes time of day, from bright gold down to sunset, and below the water to night and the moon.

Try: grab the sun and pull it into the water.

Open Sun 3Builds on now

Print

Everything is printed in dots. Project pictures appear as halftone and develop into the real thing, and the pointer changes with what it's over.

Try: move slowly down the work list. Best with a mouse.

Open Print 4Redo

Type

Big type is the motion. Your name swells where the pointer passes. The work becomes large names: point at one and it fills with its picture, with a clear copy and its line gliding alongside.

Try: run the pointer across your name.

Open Type 5Redo

Horizon

One screen, one horizon. Point at a project and the sun travels to it while its picture rises out of the water with its reflection below. Dervo comes up first.

Try: arrow keys left and right.

Open Horizon For comparison
