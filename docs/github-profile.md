Hey, I'm Bryce. I'm building [Dervo](https://trydervo.com).

Dervo runs Claude Code and Codex in your projects on your Mac, under your own logins. You type a request and it goes to the right thread. When you come back, Catch up tells you what needs you, what's still running and what finished or stopped. It writes that from the record of each run, not from the agent's account of itself. It's in private beta on macOS right now.
