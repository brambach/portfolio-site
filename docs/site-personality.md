# What "me" means for this site

Read from Bryce's own notes, not guessed: `claude-hub/personal-projects/design/TASTE.md` (28 saved references),
`design/motion/default/README.md` (the motion rules), `claude-hub/voice.md` (house style), this repo's `/previous`
site data (`src/lib/site.ts`) and its git history (the hare), and the DD portal case study. Employer-owned material
stays anonymised.

## Look

- One enormous type decision per screen, in serif display. Everything else quiet.
- Photography owns the colour. The interface stays neutral, warm and light. Dark is atmosphere (the glovebox at night), not the default.
- One or two hues and one accent, spent once. Forest and cream, with clay for small marks.
- Print DNA: paper, tape, prints, handwriting for captions. It should feel physically made.
- Never: neubrutalism (no hard offset shadows, no thick borders), neumorphism, corporate flat illustration, loud-for-loud's-sake.

## Motion

- One statement move per viewport, at most, and it plays once. Everything else is small.
- The signature overshoot curve is for stamps, pokes and creatures. Hover and press stay cheap.
- Ambient loops are slow. Name them after what they do (`hare-breath`, not `dot-3-updown`).
- Transform and opacity only. Ship the reduced-motion block.
- The creature is the hare: engraved line, sits and breathes, flicks an ear, gallops with legs matched to the ground, rests.

## Voice

- No em dashes. Contractions. Short, short, then one long one. Concrete nouns. Say what you didn't check.
- Dry, lowercase captions in first person ("clay season", "the someday car", "an operating system for exactly one person").
- Honest boundaries on every project: what survives, what was never verified. Never invent adoption, results or links.
