## Presentation direction

Let the laptop introduce the collection through strong project imagery. Opening a project could expand into a full-screen showcase, then return to the selected item in the laptop. This interaction choice is pending Bryce's preference.

Each project needs an individual visual treatment within a consistent navigation system:

1. One sentence that explains the product and one large piece of actual work.
2. One complete interaction or short sequence that a visitor can follow.
3. Two or three design or engineering decisions, each attached to the screen or behaviour it affected.
4. A clear statement of Bryce's contribution and the project's stage.
5. A direct next action, such as viewing another project, playing a recording or contacting Bryce.

Use shared case-study content for the immersive entrance and directly accessible project pages. Load media when needed, preserve keyboard focus on return, stop video when leaving and support reduced motion.

## First finish line

Build AgentSky as one complete presentation using its real interface and verified recording. Judge its opening screen, readability, interaction pacing and return to the collection at desktop and phone sizes. Use that approved quality level to develop Arro, Dervo and Lucid, then give Bryce OS, Trace and Throughline formats suited to their strongest evidence.

No project presentation has been rewritten in this audit. No new runtime, adoption, performance or production claims have been verified.
