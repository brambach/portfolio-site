Porsche 911 revving engine
jerry.berumen, Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
https://freesound.org/people/jerry.berumen/sounds/772802/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Source title: vehicle car porsche 911 revving engine motor boxer
The author identifies a Porsche 911, without a year or trim. This isn't a verified GT3 recording.
Public high-quality MP3 preview decoded to mono 44.1 kHz PCM. Excerpts supply idle (4.0-8.8 s), a sustained load bed (33.6-35.15 s, stretched to about 9.3 s), and a throttle blip (9.0-10.55 s). DC offset removal, loudness levelling, overlap crossfades and matched loop boundaries were applied. Playback pitch, filtering and gain respond to the simulated engine. A separate 5.3-second high-RPM layer holds a recorded spectrum from the same load excerpt, avoiding a repeated acceleration rise at steady redline. Processing script: scripts/prepare-911-audio.py.
