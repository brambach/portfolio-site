# Portfolio simulation

The simulation is the website at `/`. `/entrance` remains an alias. Keep the former scrolling site at `/previous` and the image experiment at `/entrance-still`. Preserve existing work. Don't push, deploy or buy assets.

## First journey: find a project in the cabin

Start above the Porsche on a forest road. Make a gentle approach to the driver's door. The handle and door invite entry, with keyboard entry available on focus and a skip option in the menu. Open the door, move through the doorway, settle in the seat and close the door. Leave a short stillness before a single fading discovery hint.

Drag or use arrow keys to look around. Pick up the laptop from the passenger seat. Lift it into the lap, open the lid and bring its physical screen into view. Project descriptions appear on that screen. Select a project, read it, go back, then close the lid and return the laptop and camera to their original positions. Keep a direct reading option for anyone who can't use the scene.

## Second journey: leave for a drive

A handwritten note near the ignition reads: “Screw this, let's just drive.” Turning the key puts the inspected object away, faces the road, plays a recorded start and settles into idle before pulling away. Entering the cabin alone never starts the engine.

Follow one real 3D forest route. Use approachable steering, acceleration, braking and parking. Open the view into warmer light at an overlook and pull over there. The cabin objects remain usable after stopping, and the key resumes the route. Mountains and city nights come later.

Recorded forest, door, ignition and engine sounds load after a deliberate gesture. Provide mute and volume. Credit each recording and describe any material sound limitations. Don't substitute harsh synthetic sounds and present them as finished audio.

## Acceptance checks

- Desktop: door entry, look around, physical laptop, project reading, natural return, key start, route, overlook, parking and return to objects.
- Keyboard: entry, menu access, projects, Escape, K for ignition, arrow keys and pedals, Space to park, sensible focus restoration.
- Touch: generous entry target, drag to look, physical screen scrolling, driving controls that release on cancellation.
- Reduced motion: skip the approach and camera travel, use an immediate parked overlook after ignition.
- Sound off, failed scene loading, hidden-tab pause and preserved `/previous` route.
- TypeScript, build, relevant state and interaction tests, then browser checks. Distinguish browser viewport checks from physical-device checks.
